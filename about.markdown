Aresys designs and builds software for synthetic aperture radar (SAR) processing and Earth observation. This site collects the parts of that work we share as open source: calibration toolboxes, data-access libraries, and the Python packages behind our EO product pipelines.

Find all of our repositories on [GitHub](https://github.com/aresys-srl), or learn more about Aresys at [aresys.it](https://www.aresys.it).
